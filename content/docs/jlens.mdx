## What is Jacobian Lens?

Jacobian Lens reads Whisper's decoder layers as if they were making predictions themselves. At each token position, it maps a layer's intermediate representation onto the vocabulary, so you can see how the model's guess develops layer by layer before it settles on the final token.

## Two operations

- **`jacobian_lens_fit`** — fits a new lens from a dataset of samples.
  Requires **2 to 1000 samples**, each clip **≤ 60 seconds** (`max_audio_seconds`
  defaults to 30), and a dataset
  or manifest that includes a **transcript** (the lens is fit against
  known text, not raw audio alone).
- **`jacobian_lens_apply`** — applies an already-fitted lens to a clip.
  Takes `top_k` (how many top results to return), an optional
  `transcript`, and `max_new_tokens` (8–256, default 64).

## The J-Lens Lab

Fitting a lens has its own dedicated page in the app: **`/j-lens`**, called
the **J-Lens Lab**. It's a 3-step flow:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Pick model and dataset">
    Choose the Whisper checkpoint and the dataset (with transcripts) you'll
    fit against.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select samples">
    Choose which samples from that dataset — within the 2–1000 range — go
    into the fit.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fit">
    Submit the fit job and wait for it to complete; the result is a saved
    lens you can apply to new clips afterward.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Saved lenses

Once fit, a lens is saved and shows up in a **saved lenses list**, ready to
apply to new clips via `jacobian_lens_apply` without re-fitting.

To remove one:

```bash
curl -X DELETE http://localhost:8000/jobs/jacobian-lenses/{lens_id} \
  --cookie "sid=<your session cookie>"
```
