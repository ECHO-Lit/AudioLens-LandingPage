## Grouping

You can slice by up to **2 grouping keys** at once (e.g. accent and
speaker together, not just one at a time).

## Minimums

A slice needs enough data before its statistics mean anything, so two
minimums are enforced:

| Setting | Default | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| `FR10_MIN_GROUP_SIZE` | 8 | minimum items in a group before it's included |
| `FR10_MIN_SPEAKERS_PER_GROUP` | 2 | minimum distinct speakers per group |

Groups below either minimum are excluded from the results rather than
reported with unreliable statistics.

## Dataset size

Fairness analysis is capped at **≤ 20,000 items total** per run.

## How long it takes

<Callout title="This is not a quick job" tone="warn">
  A full fairness run — many groups, several metrics, bootstrap resampling
  across all of them — can take **10 to 30+ minutes**. It runs on the `cpu`
  queue as a background job; submit it and come back rather than waiting
  on the request.
</Callout>

## Reading the results

- **Disparity radar** — one axis per metric, one line per group, so you can
  see at a glance which groups the model treats differently and on which
  metric.
- **Group error bars** — per-group metric values with bootstrap confidence
  intervals, so you can tell a real gap from noise.
- **Phone confusion matrix** — where phone-level annotations exist (for
  example, L2-ARCTIC), a confusion matrix of which phones get mistaken for
  which, broken out by group.
