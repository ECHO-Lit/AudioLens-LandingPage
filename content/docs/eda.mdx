## What the EDA panel shows

<Lead>
  Before you run any model on a dataset, the Dataset EDA panel lets you look
  at the dataset itself: how balanced its classes are, how its acoustic
  features are distributed, and where its outliers sit.
</Lead>

The panel covers, for a loaded dataset:

- **Class balance** — counts per label, so you can spot skew before it
  shows up as a fairness problem later.
- **Histograms** of acoustic feature distributions.
- **Box plots** to compare feature spread across classes.
- **Correlation heatmap** across acoustic features.
- **Outlier detection**, flagging clips that sit far outside the rest of the
  distribution.

## Clustering and nearest neighbours

The EDA panel also runs **HDBSCAN** clustering over the dataset's embedding
space and reports a **silhouette score** for the resulting clusters, so you
can judge how well-separated the clusters actually are rather than taking
cluster assignments at face value. From any point, you can pull up its
**nearest neighbours** in that same embedding space — useful for spotting
near-duplicates or checking whether a mislabeled clip has neighbours that
suggest what its label should have been.

This reuses the same embedding space as the [Embedding
projector](/docs/embeddings) panel — the two are looking at the same
underlying representation from different angles.

## Exporting results

| Export | Format |
|---|---|
| Acoustic features | CSV |
| Full EDA report | JSON |
| Clusters | CSV |
| Most Plotly charts | PNG download |

There is no SRT, PDF, or transcript export anywhere in AudioLens — the
exports above are the complete list.

Dataset-level EDA data is served by `GET /{dataset}/eda` for built-in
datasets; see [/docs/rest](/docs/rest) for the full endpoint reference.
